FAQ

Good questions

Is my data ever uploaded anywhere?

No. The free version stores every snapshot locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else — there's no server involved at all.

Will this get me in trouble with a store's terms of service?

No. Purchase Archive only saves pages you're already viewing in your own browser — the same way printing a page or taking a screenshot would. It doesn't log into other accounts or access anything you couldn't already see.

Can I use a saved page as legal evidence?

A saved page can support a return, warranty, or customer-service dispute, but it isn't a notarized or legally certified record. If you need something with formal evidentiary weight, pair it with a proper e-notarization service.

What happens to images that don't save properly?

A small number of images are protected by their host against being copied and may not embed. Almost everything else — text, layout, and most photos — saves correctly.

Which browsers does this work on?

Chrome and Microsoft Edge today, since both support the same extension format.
