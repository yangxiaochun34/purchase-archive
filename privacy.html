Privacy Policy

The short version: the free version of Purchase Archive doesn't collect anything. Every page you save stays inside your own browser. We don't run analytics, we don't use tracking cookies, and we have no server that could see your browsing activity or the pages you archive.

1. What we collect

Nothing, for the free version of the extension. Purchase Archive does not use analytics, does not set tracking cookies, and does not transmit your browsing history, the pages you view, or the pages you save to us or to any third party.

2. Where your data lives

Every saved product page is stored using your browser's built-in IndexedDB storage, on your device only. It never leaves your computer. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing your browser's site data for the extension, permanently deletes it — we have no copy and no way to recover it.

3. Permissions we request, and why

PermissionWhat it's used for
storageSave your archive and your settings on your own device.
downloadsLet you export a saved page as a file you can transfer to another device.
activeTab / scriptingSave the page you're currently viewing when you click the extension icon, including on sites outside our supported-store list.
tabsOpen and automatically close the background tabs used to archive items found on your order-confirmation page.
Host access to specific retailer domainsRecognize when you're viewing a supported product page so it can be archived automatically. We intentionally request access to named retailers only, not to every website you visit.

4. Optional cloud features (future)

If we introduce a paid tier with cloud sync or PDF report generation, that feature will handle data through a separate, clearly-disclosed process, and this policy will be updated before it becomes available. Nothing about the free, local-only version described above will change as a result.

5. Children's privacy

Purchase Archive is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region) — which is straightforward for us, since the free version doesn't collect information from anyone at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. If a change is material — for example, if we ever begin collecting data we don't collect today — we'll make that change clearly visible rather than adjusting it quietly.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@yourdomain.com.
