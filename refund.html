Refund Policy

The short version: the free version of Purchase Archive costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund there. This policy covers the optional paid Premium tier once it's available.

1. Free features

The core Purchase Archive extension — local automatic archiving, search, and export — is free and will remain free. Nothing in this policy applies to it.

2. Premium purchases

If you purchase a Premium one-time unlock or subscription, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date, no reason required, by emailing hello@yourdomain.com from the address used to purchase.

3. After the refund window

Requests made after the window above are considered on a case-by-case basis — for example, if a paid feature didn't work as described due to a bug on our end.

4. Subscriptions

You can cancel a Premium subscription at any time; cancelling stops future billing but doesn't retroactively refund the current billing period unless it falls within the refund window in Section 2.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method through our payment processor, typically within 5–10 business days.

6. Contact

For any refund request or question, email hello@yourdomain.com.
